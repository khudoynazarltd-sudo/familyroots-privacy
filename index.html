FamilyRoots — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

FamilyRoots is a family tree app for iPhone, Mac and Windows. Your family data belongs to you.

Data stored on your device

People, dates, relationships, places, events, notes and photos you enter are stored only on your device. FamilyRoots has no user accounts and does not upload your tree to any server.

Sharing

When you export or share a tree file, you choose which details are included (for example addresses, phone numbers or photos). Names, dates and family links are always included. Files go only to the people you send them to.

AI assistant (optional)

If you use the AI assistant, the text you type, your dictation recording or the photo you choose is sent over an encrypted connection to the FamilyRoots server and then to OpenAI to recognise names, dates and relationships. Nothing is stored on the FamilyRoots server. OpenAI processes the request under its API data policy and does not use it to train models. Nothing is added to your tree until you review and confirm it. A random device identifier is used only to apply a daily usage limit.

Places

When you look up a place, the place name is sent to Apple Maps (iPhone) or OpenStreetMap (computer) to find coordinates.

Permissions

Camera, photo library and microphone are used only when you choose to take or add a photo or dictate relatives.

Tracking and advertising

FamilyRoots does not track you, show ads, or sell data.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

Все данные семьи хранятся только на вашем устройстве. При обмене файлом вы сами выбираете, что передавать. ИИ-помощник используется только по вашему желанию: текст, диктовка или фото отправляются через сервер FamilyRoots в OpenAI для распознавания и нигде не сохраняются. Ничего не попадает в дерево без вашей проверки. Нет рекламы и слежки.
